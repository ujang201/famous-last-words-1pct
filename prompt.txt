Write the funniest possible last words a phone would say as its battery hits 1 percent, as a single line of at most 199 characters.

The line must be in the phone's own first-person voice, about its own dying battery — not a narrator, not a caption, not a slogan. It needs a real twist with the punch landing at the very end, every word earning its place, and it must feel fresh: avoid the stock moves of the genre — no will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent.

Angle to use: the phone has been a silent witness to its owner's secrets. The twist is that it is taking the deleted photos and unsent texts with it to the grave, and the punch is that the cloud — the place that was supposed to keep everything forever — gets nothing.
